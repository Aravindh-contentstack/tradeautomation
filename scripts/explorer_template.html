Strike rate counts only trades that reached the take-profit (a win) or hit the stop (a loss). Breakeven stops, 19:00 London cuts and Friday closes are excluded from it, but they are included in every R figure at their real value. Drawdown is the deepest peak-to-trough fall of the equity curve, ordered by exit time. The 19:00 breakeven rule, the Friday deadline and the one-trade-per-order-block rule are always active. Factor weights are fixed at 1.0.
